Dictate

Hold, speak, release. Or double-press to keep listening, and press once more to stop.

Reply

Replies need a writing model. Add one on the Models page whenever you want them.

  1. Select the email or message and press Ctrl+C
  2. Click into the reply box
  3. Hold the hotkey and say what you want to say, however rough
  4. A polite draft appears. Review it, then send it yourself

    Settings

    Click a box and press the combination you want. It's saved right away. Use a modifier (Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Win) with another key, two or more modifiers on their own like Ctrl+Win, or a single F-key like F8.

    Presses Enter after your words are typed, which sends them straight away in most chat apps. It only applies to dictation. Replies are always drafts for you to check.

    Installed from the Microsoft Store, wisperChad starts with Windows when you turn it on in Windows Settings → Apps → Startup.

    Reads the page's text, even parts scrolled out of view, and takes a screenshot for pictures. Keep any picture on screen when you press the hotkey, and mention details from further down when you speak. This needs a writing model that can read images, like Qwen3.5 4B. It all goes only to the writing model on your PC and is never saved.

    Signs your emails, and tells your messages from theirs: when you were the last to write, a reply follows up with them instead of answering you.

    The built-in engine needs nothing else installed. Pick Ollama if you already have it, so models don't download twice. Each engine keeps its own models.

    Send feedback

    Found a bug, or want something added? Write it here and it goes straight to the developer.

    Sends only what you write here, your email if you add it, the app's version and whether it's on Windows, Mac or Linux. It's delivered by email through Web3Forms.

    The log only records errors, never what you say or what's on your screen.

    Replies are using reply-prompts.json from the app data folder. Delete that file to go back to the built-in wording.
